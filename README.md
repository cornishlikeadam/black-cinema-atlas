## 🌟 Welcome to Black Cinema Atlas

**Black Cinema Atlas** is an independent cinematic discovery platform and cultural service dedicated to illuminating the rich, interconnected tapestry of Black film. 

Modern streaming platforms reduce cinema to sterile algorithmic feeds, buried categories, and fleeting trends. Black Cinema Atlas was created as an antidote: a fluid, dimensional cosmos where films are mapped not by corporate engagement metrics, but by genuine artistic lineage, shared emotional themes, geographical memories, and musical DNA.

Whether exploring the pioneering silent works of the 1920s, the radical brilliance of the 1970s L.A. Rebellion, the soulful poetry of 1990s romance, or the visionary heights of modern Afrofuturism, the Atlas helps you find not just what to watch next, but *why it matters*.

---

## 🌌 The Experience

### 1. The Interactive Poster Universe
- **Dual Exploratory Views:** Switch effortlessly between the fluid **Voronoi Atlas** (a cosmic, physics-driven spatial simulation) and the tactile **Poster Wall** (a curated, accessible poster gallery).
- **Lineage Clustering:** Group and align the cosmos dynamically by **Decade**, **Director**, or **Thematic Movements** (*Black Love*, *Coming of Age*, *Community*, *Afrofuturism*, *Resilience*).
- **Story Connections Graph:** Select any film to instantly trace its creative family tree—revealing shared actors, screenwriters, directors, thematic resonances, and historical parallels.

### 2. The Griot · AI Cinema Concierge
Named after the West African oral historians and storytellers, **The Griot** is your personal cinema companion:
- **Culturally Fluent Discovery:** Understands nuances across generations, from the Harlem Renaissance and Pan-African cinema to UK diaspora narratives and contemporary indie gems.
- **Spoiler-Free by Default:** Provides warm, rich context, director notes, and conversation prompts without giving away story turns.
- **Curated Recommendations:** Search by feeling, mood, historical era, or personal connection (e.g. *"A soulful love story from Chicago in the late '90s"*, *"Connect Spike Lee and Denzel Washington"*).

### 3. Movie Night · Collaborative Group Matching
Resolving the age-old *"what should we watch?"* question for couples, families, and friend groups:
- **Consensus Without Arguments:** Input individual preferences (e.g. *"Maya: music and romance; Jordan: thoughtful thriller"*), time budget, and available streaming subscriptions.
- **International Availability:** Verifies actual streaming options across the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Nigeria, South Africa, Kenya, France, and more.
- **Real-Time Group Voting:** Generate an instant, private Movie Night room link (`/night/:id`) so everyone can vote on their phones.

### 4. Art & Cinema Cultural Shop
The Atlas is paired with a curated cultural shop featuring goods that honor Black film history:
- **Fine Art & Silkscreen Prints:** Archival museum-grade prints capturing iconic cinematic imagery and visionary reinterpretations.
- **Auteur Apparel:** Heavyweight garment-dyed tees, embroidered pullovers, and cinema club totes celebrating visionary directors.
- **Books & Vinyl:** Audiophile 180g soundtrack pressings, cinematography monographs, and seminal film history texts.
- **Preservation Commitment:** **15% of all shop proceeds** directly support emerging Black independent filmmakers and archival film restoration.

The Atlas spans over a century of Black cinematic achievement across the diaspora:

```
1920s ─── Early Pioneers & Race Films (Oscar Micheaux, Evelyn Preer)
1950s ─── Mid-Century Breakthroughs (Carmen Jones, A Raisin in the Sun)

2000s ─── Global Expansion & Auteur Mastery (Love & Basketball, Tsotsi, City of God)
2010s ─── The Modern Renaissance (Moonlight, Black Panther, Get Out, Atlantics)
2020s ─── Afrofuturism, Multi-Genre Frontiers & Diaspora Horizons
```

## 🎨 Cultural Pillars & Thematic Movements

Films in the Atlas are tagged through an editorial taxonomy celebrating Black emotional and cultural life:

- **✦ Black Love:** Intimacy, romance, vulnerability, partnership, and joy (*Love Jones*, *Love & Basketball*, *If Beale Street Could Talk*, *Rye Lane*).
- **✦ Coming of Age:** Youth, self-discovery, identity, and rite-of-passage journeys (*Moonlight*, *Pariah*, *Boyz n the Hood*, *Cooley High*).
- **✦ Community & Sanctuary:** Barbershops, porches, front steps, and neighborhoods where community thrives (*Do the Right Thing*, *Barbershop*, *Friday*, *Soul Food*).
- **✦ Afrofuturism & Speculative Worlds:** Reimagining Black pasts, presents, and cosmic futures through myth, tech, and imagination (*Black Panther*, *Neptune Frost*, *Space Is the Place*, *They Cloned Tyrone*).
- **✦ Resilience & Freedom:** Historic resistance, courage, and overcoming systemic struggle (*Selma*, *Judas and the Black Messiah*, *12 Years a Slave*, *Daughters of the Dust*).
- **✦ Sonic DNA & Musicality:** The indelible rhythm of jazz, hip-hop, gospel, reggae, and afrobeat that drives Black cinema (*Summer of Soul*, *The Harder They Come*, *Mo' Better Blues*, *The Wiz*).

## 📱 Getting Started as a Viewer

1. **Visit the Atlas:** Open the live platform at [https://black-cinema-atlas.vercel.app](https://black-cinema-atlas.vercel.app) on desktop or mobile.
2. **Choose Your View:** Use the top toolbar to toggle between the **Voronoi Atlas** (cosmic spatial view) and the **Poster Wall** (grid view).
3. **Filter & Connect:** Filter by Decade, Genre, Theme, or Setting, or use the **Connect by** selector to cluster films by director or shared motifs.
4. **Consult The Griot:** Tap **Ask the guide** at any point for bespoke recommendations, film pairings, or background notes.
5. **Save to Watchlist:** Bookmark films to keep a persistent watchlist saved locally on your device without needing a password.
6. **Visit the Shop:** Explore limited-edition prints and cultural goods in the **Art & Shop** tab.

## 📜 Ethical Attribution & Cultural Stewardship

- **Film Metadata & Imagery:** Film credits, synopsis, and imagery are aggregated via TMDB. Streaming availability data is supplied by JustWatch. This product uses the TMDB API but is not certified or endorsed by TMDB.
- **Engine Foundations:** The cosmic Voronoi simulation builds on open WebGL physics concepts pioneered in the creative coding community, adapted specifically for cinematic poster textures and lineage clustering.
- **Preservation Mission:** 15% of shop revenue is dedicated to independent archiving and preservation of Black cinema titles.
